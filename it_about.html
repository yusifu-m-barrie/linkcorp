About Us

LINK CORPORATION LIMITED is a team of young IT professionals working in different IT fields, as part of the LINK network. Our services range from website designing, software development, database development, computer repairs; networking; electronic data collection; ICT trainings to mobile application among others. As we are very passionate about our work, we decided to create LINK CORPORATION to expand our services and have the opportunity to help as many people as possible. Our aim is to always find a solution for your IT related problem within the shortest possible TIME by providing quality service at a reasonable cost. We support residential computer users as well as small businesses, government institutions and companies across all sectors. We are confident that you will be satisfied with the outstanding quality of our work. We work on basis - No Fix, No Fee policy so you only get charged if we can solve the problem.

  • Our Mission:

    • To improve organizations and individual performance through IT.

  • Our Values:
