Action For Advocacy & Development (AAD S/L)

Action for Advocacy and Development – Sierra Leone (AAD-S/L) grows from the exit process of War Child Holland (an International Non-Governmental Organization from the Netherlands which has operated in Sierra Leone since 2001 till 2012). War Child Holland started its intervention in Sierra Leone after the decade civil war with creative workshops for children and teachers, implemented in primary Schools and relief centres for ex-child soldiers, sexually abused girls, war-wounded, orphans.

Gamaliel Christian University (GCU)

Gamaliel Christian University worldwide was incepted in Cameroun in 2004 during which period our Chancellor Apostle Dr. Samuel J. Williams was a refugee as a result of Civil war in Liberia. Since then his passion to increase the level of knowledge among ministers of the gospel has taken him to several nations in Africa. As a result Gamaliel now has four functional campuses in Liberia, Sierra Leone, Zambia, and Malawi with advance school of Ministers in over Twelve (12) other African nations.

Sierra Leone on the Right to Food (SiLNoRF)

TThe Sierra Leone Network on the Right to Food (SiLNoRF) is a consortium of Civil Society, Local NGOs and Community Based Organizations operating in Sierra Leone. SiLNoRF is registered as a Not for Profit organization with the Office of the Registrar General of Companies in Sierra Leone. SiLNoRF is a member of the African Network on the Right to Food (ANoRF). LINK Corporation was hired to design and develop a well functional website for them.
